# Identity

Identity is the foundation of ConnectID.

## The principle

A digital identity should not exist only as an account inside a platform.

ConnectID explores a model where an individual's digital identity can become a persistent part of their participation in a digital society.

## Identity and ownership

The individual should be at the center of their digital identity.

ConnectID therefore explores identity around:

- Individual ownership
- Persistence
- Portability
- Security
- Transparency

## Identity as a foundation

Identity connects the different parts of ConnectID.

A Citizen's identity can become the foundation for:

- Community participation
- Reputation
- Contributions
- Relationships
- Ecosystem participation

The technical implementation will be developed gradually.

The goal is to establish the right architecture before implementing advanced functionality.
