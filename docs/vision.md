# ConnectID Vision

## Own your identity. Build your reputation.

ConnectID is an experiment in building a parallel digital society.

A digital environment where people can own their identity, participate as Citizens and build reputation through meaningful contribution.

Everyone starts as a Citizen.

Identity belongs to the individual.

Reputation is earned.

Contribution matters.

ConnectID is not intended to be another social network or simply another token.

It is an exploration of what a digital society could look like when identity, reputation and community are connected.

## The foundation

ConnectID is built around four fundamental ideas:

- Identity
- Citizens
- Reputation
- Community

These elements form the foundation on which the wider ConnectID ecosystem can develop.

## Identity

Your digital identity should not belong to a platform.

ConnectID explores an identity that can become a persistent part of your digital presence.

## Citizens

Everyone starts as a Citizen.

A Citizen is a participant in the ConnectID ecosystem.

Citizens can build their identity, participate in the community and contribute to the ecosystem.

## Reputation

Reputation is earned over time.

Meaningful participation and contribution can become part of a Citizen's reputation.

The reputation system will be developed gradually and transparently.

## Community

Community is where the ConnectID concept becomes real.

Citizens should eventually be able to communicate, collaborate and build relationships within the ecosystem.

## The experiment

ConnectID is an experiment.

Not every mechanism is defined yet.

The project will evolve through development, testing and community participation.

The objective is to build something useful rather than simply create another platform.
