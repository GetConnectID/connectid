# Citizens

Everyone starts as a Citizen.

## What is a Citizen?

A Citizen is a participant in the ConnectID digital society.

A Citizen is not simply a username or account.

The Citizen concept connects identity, participation and reputation.

## Starting point

Every participant begins as a Citizen.

There is no requirement to start with influence, status or reputation.

Reputation can be built through participation and contribution.

## Citizen development

As ConnectID develops, Citizens may be able to:

- Establish their identity
- Create a profile
- Participate in the community
- Contribute to the ecosystem
- Build reputation
- Connect with other Citizens

## The principle

The Citizen system should encourage participation rather than purchased status.

The exact mechanisms will be developed and tested as the project evolves.
