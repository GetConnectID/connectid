# Reputation

Reputation is earned.

## The principle

ConnectID explores a reputation system based on meaningful participation and contribution.

A Citizen's reputation should reflect what they contribute to the ecosystem rather than simply how many followers they have.

## Potential reputation inputs

The system may eventually consider:

- Contributions
- Community participation
- Collaboration
- Verified achievements
- Useful activity
- Positive contribution to other Citizens

These are concepts, not finalized rules.

## What reputation should not become

The reputation system should avoid becoming a simple popularity score.

Quantity alone should not determine reputation.

The goal is to create a system where meaningful contribution matters.

## Development

The reputation model will be designed and tested gradually.

Transparency will be important as the system evolves.
