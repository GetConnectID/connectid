# ConnectID Ecosystem

ConnectID is designed as a layered ecosystem.

## Core layers

```text
Identity
   ↓
Citizen
   ↓
Reputation
   ↓
Community
   ↓
Digital Society
Identity

The identity layer establishes who a Citizen is within the ecosystem.

Citizen

The Citizen layer represents participation in the ConnectID digital society.

Reputation

The reputation layer represents what a Citizen has earned through contribution and participation.

Community

The community layer provides the environment where Citizens interact and contribute.

Digital Society

The long-term objective is to connect these layers into a wider digital society.

CNID

CNID can provide an economic layer for the ecosystem where appropriate.

CNID is therefore considered supporting infrastructure rather than the central purpose of ConnectID.

Development principle

Each layer should be useful independently while remaining capable of connecting to the other layers.

The architecture should allow ConnectID to evolve without requiring the entire system to be rebuilt every time a new component is introduced.
