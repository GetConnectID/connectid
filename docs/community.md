# Community

The community is where ConnectID becomes practical.

## The goal

ConnectID should eventually provide an environment where Citizens can interact, communicate and contribute.

## Community functionality

The future ConnectID application is expected to explore functionality such as:

- Citizen profiles
- Communication
- Discussions
- Direct interaction
- Community participation
- Contribution tracking
- Reputation development

## Communication

Communication is an important part of the ConnectID ecosystem.

The future application can include a native communication layer rather than depending entirely on external platforms.

The exact implementation will be determined during technical development.

## Community first

The community should be built around participation.

The objective is not to create another platform optimized for attention.

The objective is to create a place where Citizens can participate in the digital society being developed by ConnectID.
