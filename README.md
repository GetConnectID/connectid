# ConnectID

## Own your identity. Build your reputation.

ConnectID is an experiment in building a parallel digital society.

A digital environment where people can own their identity, participate as Citizens and build reputation through meaningful contribution.

Everyone starts as a Citizen.

Your identity is yours.
Your reputation is earned.
Your contribution matters.

---

## What is ConnectID?

The internet gave everyone the ability to create digital accounts and profiles.

But most digital identities are still controlled by the platforms we use.

ConnectID explores a different model.

A digital society where identity can belong to the individual, where people participate as Citizens, and where reputation is built through contribution.

ConnectID is not simply another social network.

It is an experiment in what a digital society could become.

---

## The foundation

ConnectID is built around four core concepts:

- **Identity** — a persistent digital identity belonging to the individual
- **Citizens** — participants in the ConnectID ecosystem
- **Reputation** — something earned through meaningful contribution
- **Community** — the environment where Citizens interact and participate

These foundations can evolve into a wider digital ecosystem.

---

## Identity

Your digital identity should not be limited to a collection of accounts owned by different platforms.

ConnectID explores an identity layer designed around the individual.

The identity layer will form the foundation for the rest of the ecosystem.

---

## Citizens

Everyone starts as a Citizen.

A Citizen is a participant in the ConnectID digital society.

Citizens can build their identity, participate in the community and contribute to the ecosystem.

There is no requirement to begin with status or influence.

Participation creates the opportunity to build reputation.

---

## Reputation

Reputation is earned over time.

Meaningful contribution, participation and collaboration can become part of a Citizen's reputation.

The exact mechanisms will be developed gradually and transparently.

The objective is to reward meaningful contribution rather than simply popularity or activity.

---

## Community

The ConnectID community will be one of the first practical layers of the ecosystem.

Citizens will eventually be able to:

- Create and manage their profile
- Connect with other Citizens
- Communicate
- Participate in discussions
- Contribute to the ecosystem
- Build reputation through participation

The community is not separate from ConnectID.

It is part of the experiment.

---

## The ecosystem

The initial concept can be represented as:

Identity
↓
Citizen
↓
Reputation
↓
Community
↓
Digital Society

Each layer builds on the previous one.

The system will be developed gradually rather than attempting to build everything at once.

---

## CNID

CNID is intended to function as fuel for the ConnectID ecosystem.

The token is not the purpose of ConnectID.

The ecosystem is the purpose.

As ConnectID develops, CNID may become connected to specific ecosystem functions and participation mechanisms.

Those mechanisms will only be introduced when they have a meaningful role within the ecosystem.

ConnectID is being developed from the ground up.

### `docs/`

The concepts, architecture and documentation behind ConnectID.

### `app/`
